Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Thank you for playing Mecha Wild. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our app. We believe in keeping things simple and honest.

🔒 The Short Version

✅ No data collected

✅ No account required

✅ No internet required

✅ No ads

✅ No tracking

Mecha Wild is a self-contained offline game. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Ever.

📋 Information We Collect

We collect no personal information whatsoever. Mecha Wild does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal details to play. There is no registration process.

📡 Internet Access

Mecha Wild does not require an internet connection to function. The game runs entirely on your device. No data is sent to external servers, and no network requests are made during gameplay.

📊 Analytics & Tracking

We do not use any analytics services, crash reporting tools, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking software. Your gameplay behaviour, session duration, and in-game actions are never monitored or recorded.

📢 Advertising

Mecha Wild contains no advertisements. We do not integrate any ad networks (such as AdMob, Facebook Audience Network, or similar). No advertising identifiers are accessed or shared.

💾 Data Storage

Any game progress or settings (such as save files or preferences) are stored locally on your device only, using standard Android storage. This data never leaves your device and is only accessible to you.

👶 Children's Privacy

Mecha Wild does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected at all, the game is safe for all ages.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Any significant changes will also be noted in the app's Play Store update notes.
